The issuer's filenames for RCYS and RPYS are transposed; the actual artwork reads RCYS and RPYS.
RYS / RCYS / RPYS remain school marks. Displaying a user-entered school designation does not convert it to a personal RYT credential or verify school ownership.

A generic undated mark is used rather than a holder-specific image stating a registration year.

iyengar-certification-mark.png is the unmodified Iyengar Yoga Certification Mark from Iyengar Yoga UK:
https://iyengaryoga.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/CM-logo-IY-UK-01-1024x703-1.png
The mark is trademark protected and is intended only for certified Iyengar teachers who are licensed/current under the applicable association rules. Polish certification references:
https://joga.org.pl/certyfikat-marki.html and https://joga.org.pl/stopnie-nauczycielskie.html

The shared matcher accepts common punctuation, spacing and descriptive titles around a supported designation. Iyengar / Iyengara / CIYT are recognized with or without a level, including Polish names such as "Joga Iyengara" or "Certyfikat nauczyciela jogi Iyengara".
Unspecified YA levels, unsupported credentials and titles naming multiple different credentials use the normal upload/neutral fallback instead of guessing a designation.

Display is based on the instructor-entered name and does not verify the credential or active status. Uploaded images take priority over default artwork in both the editor and public card; documents remain accessible through the existing dialog. Automatic marks are local assets and never stored in image_id.
